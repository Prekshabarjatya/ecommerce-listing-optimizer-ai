# Company & Brand

Source: Santerra Hygiene master knowledge base (Company Fundamentals, Vision/Mission/Values, Brand Positioning, Founder, Marketing Core Messages, Unique Selling Propositions).

## Basic Information

- **Company Name:** Santerra Hygiene Private Limited
- **Headquarters:** MZ–9, Bansi Trade Centre, New Palasia, Indore – 452001, Madhya Pradesh, India
- **Founded:** August 2025
- **Industry:** FMCG Hygiene & Household Essentials
- **Business Model:** B2C and B2B

## Brand Tagline

**"Hygiene for Everyone"**

## Brand Promise

**"Premium Quality at the Right Price"**

## Vision Statement

To become India's most trusted and sustainable hygiene brand with a global footprint by delivering eco-friendly, affordable, and high-quality hygiene solutions that improve health, cleanliness, well-being, and environmental responsibility for every individual.

## Mission Statement

To deliver safe, affordable, and eco-conscious hygiene and personal care products for modern households by focusing on sustainability, product quality, innovation, and customer trust.

## Core Values (6 Pillars)

1. **Softness** - Gentle, comfortable, skin-friendly materials
2. **Purity** - Non-negotiable product safety; free from harmful chemicals
3. **Sustainability First** - Biodegradable materials, responsible sourcing
4. **Transparency** - Honest claims, clear labeling, ingredient transparency
5. **Quality You Can Feel** - Reliable performance, superior experience
6. **Innovation** - Continuous improvement in materials, formulations, packaging

## Positioning Statement

Santerra Hygiene is an affordable premium hygiene brand that provides safe, eco-conscious, and high-quality hygiene essentials for modern Indian households. The brand bridges the gap between expensive premium hygiene products and low-quality budget alternatives.

## Brand Archetypes

- **Primary:** Caregiver (caring, trustworthy, protective, supportive, family-oriented)

## Founder: Mr. DV

15+ years of experience across staple fiber, nonwoven fabrics, wet wipes, and tissue manufacturing, in India, the US, Germany, Thailand, and Saudi Arabia. Santerra is his 11th venture. Growth targets: ₹30 crore brand by 2030, ₹100 crore company by 2035.

## Unique Selling Propositions

1. **Complete Hygiene Ecosystem** - Tissue, wipes, baby care, mother care, cleaning, food safety under one brand
2. **Affordable Premium Quality** - High quality without premium pricing
3. **Eco-Conscious** - Biodegradable materials, sustainable sourcing, responsible manufacturing
4. **Made in India** - Reducing import dependency, creating local jobs
5. **Family-Centric** - Designed for modern Indian households
6. **Transparency-Driven** - Clear communication, honest claims, ingredient transparency
7. **Omnichannel Availability** - Online (Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho) and offline (retail, distributors)
8. **Growth Through In-House Manufacturing** - Moving from imports to own production by 2026-2028
