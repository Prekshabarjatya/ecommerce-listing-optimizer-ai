# Sustainability Strategy & Approved Claims

Source: Santerra Hygiene master knowledge base (Sustainability Strategy). This is the
primary reference for validating environmental/sustainability claims in generated
listing copy — a later Fact Validation Agent stage should check generated claims
against the approved list below before they ship.

## Sustainability Vision

To become India's most trusted and sustainable hygiene brand by making environmentally responsible hygiene products accessible to every household.

## Key Sustainability Initiatives

### 1. Biodegradable Materials

- Wet wipes: Biodegradable nonwoven fabrics, plant-derived fibers
- Baby wipes: Skin-safe biodegradable materials
- Packaging: Reduced plastic dependency, recyclable alternatives
- **Approved Claims:** "Designed with sustainability in mind," "Moving toward biodegradable solutions," "Responsible material choices"

### 2. FSC Alignment

- Support responsible forestry and sustainable paper sourcing
- Applied to tissue products (facial, toilet, kitchen tissue, napkins) and paper-based packaging
- **Approved Messaging:** "Responsibly sourced paper," "FSC-aligned sourcing approach"

### 3. Responsible Sourcing

**Supplier Evaluation Criteria:** Product quality and compliance, environmental responsibility, ethical practices, reliability.

**Priority Materials:** Sustainable pulp sourcing, biodegradable nonwoven fabrics, recyclable packaging components

### 4. Sustainable Packaging

- Material reduction (optimized pack sizes, efficient utilization)
- Recyclability (recyclable paper, cartons, reduced mixed-material packaging)
- Consumer education (disposal guidance, recycling instructions)

### 5. Environmental Claims Policy

**Communication Principles:** Accuracy, transparency, clarity, compliance

**Approved Claim Categories:**

- Materials: Biodegradable materials, responsibly sourced paper
- Packaging: Recyclable components, reduced waste initiatives
- Manufacturing: Sustainability-focused improvements, responsible sourcing

**Claims Requiring Verification (do not use without proof):** Fully biodegradable, compostable, carbon neutral, zero waste, plastic-free, 100% sustainable
