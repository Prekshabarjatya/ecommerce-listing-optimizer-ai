# Customer Segments

Source: Santerra Hygiene master knowledge base (Customer Segments).

## Tier 1 (Primary Revenue Drivers)

1. **Parents** (Age 25-45, prioritize safety, quality, affordability)
2. **Mothers** (Key decision-makers for household hygiene)
3. **Homemakers** (Daily household managers, budget-conscious)

## Tier 2 (Growth Segments)

1. **Caregivers** (Family & professional caregivers)
2. **Retailers** (Kirana stores, medical stores, supermarkets)
3. **Distributors** (Regional FMCG distribution partners)

## Tier 3 (Strategic Segments)

1. **Institutional Buyers** (Hospitals, schools, hotels, offices)
2. **Wellness Brands** (Spas, salons, healthcare brands)

## Universal Customer Needs

Safety, Trust, Affordability, Quality, Convenience, Hygiene, Sustainability, Reliability
