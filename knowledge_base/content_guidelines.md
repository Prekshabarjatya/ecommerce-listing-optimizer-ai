# Website & Content Strategy Guidelines

Source: Santerra Hygiene master knowledge base (Website & Content Strategy). Reference
for what generated listing/marketing copy should and should not contain.

## Website Tone

Clean, family-oriented, trustworthy, product-focused, educational, reassuring

## Content Objectives

Every content piece should achieve at least one of:

1. Educate
2. Build Trust
3. Demonstrate Product Benefits
4. Promote Hygiene Awareness
5. Encourage Sustainable Living
6. Strengthen Brand Recall

## Content Formula

Problem → Education → Solution → Benefit → Trust Signal

## Approved Content Themes

Hygiene education, family wellness, baby care, home care, sustainable living, cleaning tips, food safety, household organization, product usage, eco-friendly practices

## Content to Avoid

Medical claims, disease prevention claims, fear-based marketing, competitor attacks, unrealistic promises, unverified sustainability claims
