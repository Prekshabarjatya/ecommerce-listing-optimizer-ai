# Marketing Strategy & Sales Channels

Source: Santerra Hygiene master knowledge base (Marketing Strategy, Sales & Distribution).

## Marketing Mission

To establish Santerra as India's most trusted and accessible hygiene brand by delivering valuable, educational, and customer-focused experiences across every touchpoint.

## Marketing Pillars

- Trust & Transparency
- Family Care
- Hygiene Education
- Sustainability
- Affordable Premium Quality
- Everyday Convenience

## Marketing Funnel

Awareness → Interest → Consideration → Purchase → Retention → Advocacy

## SEO (Long-term Growth Channel)

**Key Content Clusters:** Tissue & Paper Products, Wet Wipes, Baby Wipes (alcohol-free, gentle, newborn), Food Safety (aluminum foil, food wrapping paper), Home Hygiene.

**Content Types:** Blogs, product pages, buying guides, comparison articles, FAQ pages

## E-Commerce Marketplaces

- **Amazon India** (primary platform)
- **Flipkart** (value-conscious consumers)
- **Meesho** (Tier 2/3 households)
- **Expansion:** JioMart, BigBasket, Blinkit, Zepto, Swiggy Instamart

## Phase 1 Focus (Current)

1. Amazon
2. Flipkart
3. Meesho
4. SEO
5. Social Media

## Sales Channels

### B2C (Direct-to-Consumer)

Official website, social commerce, WhatsApp commerce, future mobile app.
**KPIs:** Website traffic, conversion rate, AOV, CAC, repeat purchase rate, CLV

### Retail Sales

General Trade (Kirana, general, medical stores), Specialty Retail (baby stores, hygiene stores).
**Support:** POS materials, retail branding, product training, promotional schemes

### Distribution Sales

FMCG distributors, hygiene distributors, regional stockists.
**Model:** Manufacturer → Distributor → Retailer → Consumer

### Modern Trade

Reliance Smart, D-Mart, More, Spencer's, Metro, Vishal Mega Mart.
**Focus:** National visibility, category leadership, high-volume sales

### Marketplace Sales

Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho (primary). JioMart, BigBasket, Blinkit, Zepto, Swiggy Instamart (expansion).

### Institutional Sales

Healthcare, Education, Hospitality, Corporate, Government.

### Export Sales (2027 onwards)

**Phase 1 Markets:** UAE, Saudi Arabia, Oman, Qatar, Bahrain
**Phase 2:** Africa, Southeast Asia, South Asia
**Phase 3:** Europe, North America
