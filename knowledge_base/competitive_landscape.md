# Competitive Landscape

Source: Santerra Hygiene master knowledge base (Competitive Landscape).

## Major Competitors by Category

### Baby Care

- Pampers (P&G) - Premium, trusted, hospital-recommended
- Huggies (Kimberly-Clark) - Comfort-focused, global brand
- Himalaya - Natural/herbal positioning
- Mother Sparsh - Water-wipes, digital-savvy, premium

### Tissue & Paper

- Origami - Category specialist, premium positioning
- Premier Tissue - Value-focused
- Selpak, Bella Noce

### Feminine Hygiene

- Whisper (P&G) - Category leader, premium
- Stayfree - Strong awareness, affordable
- Sofy - Innovation-focused
- Bella - International credibility

### Adult Care

- Friends Adult Diapers - Category leader
- Lifree - Premium positioning
- Seni

## Santerra's Competitive Advantages

1. **Complete Hygiene Ecosystem** - Multiple categories under one brand (competitors focus on single categories)
2. **Affordable Premium Positioning** - Premium quality without premium pricing
3. **Sustainability Focus** - Responsible sourcing, FSC alignment, biodegradable initiatives
4. **Family-Centric Brand** - Built around modern Indian households, not just one category
5. **Emerging Challenger Brand** - Agile, innovative, capable of adapting faster than incumbents
