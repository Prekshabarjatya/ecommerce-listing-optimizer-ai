# Operations & Strategic Roadmap

Source: Santerra Hygiene master knowledge base (Operations, 5-Year Strategic Roadmap,
Key Financial Metrics, Quick Reference).

## Manufacturing

- Tissue & Paper Products
- Wet Wipes & Baby Wipes
- Home Hygiene Products
- Family Care Products (diapers, pads)

**Strategic Manufacturing Plan:**

- **Dec 2026:** Own automatic tissue manufacturing unit (20-ton monthly capacity)
- **2028:** GMP-certified personal & baby care production factory

## Packaging

- Clean, minimalist design reflecting brand values
- Information hierarchy: Brand name → Product name → Key benefit → Quantity → Instructions
- Focus on recyclability and sustainability

## Quality Control

**Quality Parameters:**

- Softness, absorbency, strength (tissue products)
- Moisture content, fabric quality, seal integrity (wipes)
- Absorbency, comfort, leak protection, material safety (hygiene products)

**Quality Checkpoints:** Raw material inspection → In-process inspection → Finished goods inspection

## Certifications (Current & Future)

- ISO 9001 (Quality Management)
- ISO 14001 (Environmental Management)
- GMP Compliance
- Food Contact Safety Standards
- FSC-Aligned Sourcing

## 5-Year Strategic Roadmap (2025-2030)

### 2025 (August - Current)

- Company establishment and launch
- Launch Paperly tissue range
- Launch Santerra wipes/cleaners initial range

### 2026

- **Jan-Mar:** Establish distributor network in MP, Gujarat, Rajasthan, Chhattisgarh
- **Feb:** Launch premium diapers (M, L, XL) & sanitary pads
- **July:** Launch complete baby care range (oil, soap, lotion, shampoo)
- **Dec:** Commence own tissue manufacturing facility (20-ton monthly capacity) in Indore

### 2027

- Start GCC market exports (UAE, Saudi Arabia, etc.)
- Expand institutional sales network
- Full portfolio availability across all channels

### 2028

- Launch own GMP-certified personal & baby care production factory

### 2030 (Ultimate Goal)

- ₹100 Crore annual turnover
- Fully integrated hygiene campus established
- Strong market leadership position in India
- Established export presence
